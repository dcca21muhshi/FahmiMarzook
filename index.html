Even now, I still can’t fully believe that you are mine. I don’t really know how to put into words just how much I love and adore you. That is my biggest wish, my most heartfelt dua, and the one thing I will always pray for — you and me, together, happy, and by each other’s side until the very end.

WORDS FROM MY HEART

A Little Letter For You ♡

♡

My Love,

Happy Wedding Monthasary! ♡

Alhamdulillah… two months of us. 🤍🌙 It’s hard to believe that it has already been two months. Two months may sound like such a small amount of time, but when I look back at everything we’ve shared, it honestly feels like you’ve been a part of my heart for much longer. Somewhere between our conversations, the little moments, the laughter, missing each other, and all those simple things that probably mean nothing to anyone else, you became someone incredibly important to me. ❤️ I don’t think I can perfectly explain what you mean to me. You have become the person I want to tell things to, the person I look forward to talking to, the person who can change my mood just by being there. Even on ordinary days, somehow, having you in my life makes them feel a little more special. And honestly, I love the little things about us the most. The way we talk, the way we understand each other, the silly conversations, the moments when we miss each other, and even those tiny disagreements that remind me how much I actually care about you. Every little memory we’re creating is becoming something I quietly keep in my heart. 🥹❤️ You’ve given me a kind of happiness I didn’t know I was missing. And sometimes I catch myself smiling because of something you said or did, and I just think, “Alhamdulillah… I have this person in my life.” 🤍 But my love for you isn’t only about what I feel today. I want to love you in a way that is pleasing to Allah. I want our bond to bring us closer to Him, not further away. I want whatever Allah has written for us to be beautiful, pure, peaceful, and blessed. So I make dua for you. I make dua for us. 🤲🏻🌙 Ya Allah, protect the person I love. Protect their heart, their happiness, their faith, and their dreams. Keep them safe wherever they are. Put barakah in their life and guide them toward everything that is good for them. And if we are truly written for each other, make our path toward one another easy, halal, and beautiful. Let our love grow with patience, understanding, respect, and deen. I don’t know what tomorrow holds. I don’t know how many chapters Allah has written for us. But I know that these past two months have given me memories I’ll always be grateful for. And if Allah allows me to continue this journey with you, I want to keep making memories with you—more laughter, more late conversations, more silly moments, more understanding, more growth, and, InshaAllah, a future where we can look back and say, “Alhamdulillah, we made it through everything together.” 🤍

But somehow, even with all these miles between us, you still manage to feel so close to my heart. We may not be able to hold each other’s hands, sit beside each other, or meet whenever we want, but your presence still reaches me through every message, every call, every “I miss you,” and every little moment we share from far away. I miss the little things about you—the things I can’t experience through a screen. I miss the thought of being able to look at you and smile without saying anything. I miss imagining the day when we won’t have to say “goodnight” through a phone and wish we could stay a little longer. Until then, I’ll keep cherishing every call, every message, every laugh, every late conversation, and every little memory we create from miles apart. 🫶🏻❤️ Sometimes I wish I could close the distance with just one step and find you right beside me. But until Allah makes that possible, I’ll keep you close in my duas and in my heart. 🤲🏻🌙

Happy 2nd month anniversary, my love. ❤️🫶🏻 Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for being you. Thank you for every little moment you’ve given me. And thank you for becoming a part of my heart without even realizing how deeply you were settling there. Two months have passed, but my heart already has so many memories with you. I don’t just want you for a moment. I pray that, if Allah has written us for each other, I get to keep choosing you, caring for you, praying for you, and loving you through every chapter that comes next. 🤍🌙 You are one of the most beautiful things I’ve ever had the chance to thank Allah for. Alhamdulillah for the day our paths crossed. Alhamdulillah for these two months. And InshaAllah, Alhamdulillah for everything still waiting for us. ❤️🌙 Happy two months, my love. You have a very special place in my heart—one I hope Allah keeps protected. 🤍

Here's to us, our memories, and all the beautiful chapters still to come. ♡

With all my love,
Your Wife ♡
